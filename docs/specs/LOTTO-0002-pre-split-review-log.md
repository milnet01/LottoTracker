# LOTTO-0002 — pre-split cold-eyes review log (archived)

**This is a record, not a contract.** It holds the three cold-eyes loops run
between 2026-08-02 and 2026-08-02 against the **undivided** LOTTO-0002 spec — a
1,161-line document covering the page, its security boundary, the tray and the
supervisor, which no longer exists.

That document was split twice on 2026-08-02, into
`LOTTO-0002-local-web-page.md` (the model, the refresh lifecycle and what the
page shows), `LOTTO-0014-http-surface-and-security.md` (the HTTP surface and
the security boundary) and `LOTTO-0013-tray-and-supervisor.md` (the tray, the
supervisor and the headless contract).

**These loops confer no review credit on any of the three.** They were run
against bytes none of them now has, and each part re-enters the gate at loop 1
on its own. The rows are kept because deleting them would destroy the audit
trail of who looked and what they found — 83 verified findings across three
loops, 0 deferred — and because the reasoning in them is the best available
record of *why* several rules in the successor specs read the way they do.

They are archived out of the spec itself for one measured reason: at 10,917
bytes they were 15% of the post-split page spec, and every cold-eyes loop pays
to read the whole file. History an implementer never reads should not be
charged to every future review.
